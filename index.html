01 · Room

A room, a wall, a seed.

A work is drawn at the moment of its escape. A cellular law grows the cluster generation by generation; the drawing freezes at the first generation that puts three cells beyond the wall, with the gap torn exactly where the pattern came through. Works are shown below from demonstration seeds, drawn by the same code that draws the edition.

Six laws are in the edition: Life, HighLife, Day & Night, Coral, Maze and Mazectric. Twelve palettes. One seed per work, fixed on-chain at mint, so any work can be redrawn by anyone, forever, and always comes out the same.

02 · Fates

Two fates.

Status is a trait recorded in every work's metadata, alongside the breached side, the law, the palette and the seed. Nobody chooses it. A wallet cannot mint its way to a Held on purpose; it can only keep minting and take what the seeds give.

03 · Mint

Only agents may mint.

Minting accepts one caller: a Binance Agentic Wallet holding an Agent ID. Ordinary wallets and other contracts are turned away on-chain. One work per call, at most twenty works per wallet, free to mint: any anti-Sybil value, if one applies, is shown in your wallet before you sign and never on this page.

04 · Claim

Every work claims $LIB.

Every minted Liberate carries a claim of $LIB, paid once per work. How much depends on where the work stood in its first wallet: the earlier a work was minted by that wallet, the smaller its claim; the works a wallet minted last carry the largest claims in the edition. Sell a work before claiming and the claim travels with it, at the value fixed by its first wallet.

Claims run through your Binance agent, exactly like the mint, and carry no claim fee. Claims open once the claim pool is funded on-chain; the ledger below reads the contract directly.

Notes
Why only agents?

Liberate is about rules that run without a hand on them, so it is minted by software that acts on its own. The contract requires the SPACE ID Agent ID that Binance Agentic Wallets claim, and rejects calls from contracts.

What decides if my work gets out?

A seed made from the block and your address at mint. It picks the law, the palette, the starting cluster and the growth. Run the growth and the answer is whatever happens: through the wall, or held. The same seed always draws the same work.

Why is a later mint worth more $LIB?

Claims reward depth in one wallet. A wallet that mints once holds a small claim; a wallet that mints twenty holds claims worth 66,399,980 $LIB in total. The amount is fixed at mint by the work's order in that wallet and never changes, whoever owns the work later.

Where does it live?

Seeds live on BNB Chain. Drawing code is open and runs in this page, so anyone can redraw any Liberate from its seed. Metadata and artwork files are served from this site.
